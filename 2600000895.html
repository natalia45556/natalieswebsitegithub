How to prepare Coffee

  • Boil fresh,clean water
  • Measure the required amount of coffee powder
  • Add hot water to the coffee powder
  • Stir well and allow the coffee to brew
  • Add milk and sugar according to your preference
  • Serve the coffe while hot
